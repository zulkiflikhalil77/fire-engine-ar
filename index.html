Fire Engine AR

Drag to turn · Pinch to zoom · Tap a number to pick a mechanism

AR isn’t available here. Open this lesson on a phone or iPad in Safari or Chrome to place the fire engine on your desk.

Using AR

  1. Tap View on your desk.
  2. Move your device slowly over the table until the fire engine appears.
  3. Drag to move it. Pinch to make it bigger or smaller.
  4. Put the real toy next to it and compare the parts.

Using Camera mode

  1. Tap Camera and allow camera access.
  2. Point your device at your desk. The fire engine appears over the camera view.
  3. Tap the numbers or the buttons at the bottom, press Play, and turn on X-ray.
  4. Tap Exit camera to return to the lesson.

Android phones play the animations in AR. iPhone and iPad show the model still in AR, so play each animation in the 3D view first.

Gear sizes in this model are chosen for teaching and may differ from your toy.
